Privacy Notice

For contractors and viewers on a PenLog project

Last updated: November 2026

In short

  • PenLog records fire-boundary penetration work on a ship in drydock, as evidence for the ship's safety records.
  • If you log work with a PenLog link, PenLog keeps your name, your company, what you recorded and the photos you took. If you add your email, it is used only to tell you about your own pens.
  • The company running the project (usually the ship operator) decides what happens to this data. Whitebridge Marine runs PenLog for them.
  • We do not sell your data, show you adverts or track you across other websites.

Who is responsible

The company running the drydock project that invited you (for example, the ship operator) is the controller of your data. It decides why the work is recorded and how long the records are kept.

Whitebridge Marine Kft. (company registration number 01-09-448318, Belgrád rakpart 26. 4. em. 3.B. ajtó, 1056 Budapest, Hungary) provides PenLog and processes the data on that company's instructions. You can contact us at info@whitebridgemarine.com.

What PenLog keeps

If you are a contractor using a PenLog link

  • Your name as written on your link, and your company.
  • The penetrations you record: deck, fire zone, frame, location, what passes through, the seal used, and when you opened and sealed them.
  • The photos you take, with the time they were taken.
  • Messages between you and the supervisor about your pens.
  • Your email address, only if you or your supervisor add it.
  • When your link was last used.

If you are a viewer or supervisor with an account

  • Your name or username, your email address and the projects you can see.
  • A record of the changes you make, such as verifying a pen or inviting a viewer, kept in the project's audit trail.

For everyone

  • Technical logs kept by our hosting providers for security, which include your IP address.
  • Anonymous usage statistics that do not include your name or your link.

Why

  • To keep an accurate record of who opened and sealed each fire-boundary penetration, and when, for the ship's safety management and the close-out report.
  • To tell you when the supervisor needs something on one of your pens, if you have given an email address.
  • To keep PenLog secure and working.

The company running the project decides the legal basis for this, usually its legitimate interest in keeping complete safety records for the ship.

Where it is stored

PenLog runs on established cloud providers. The application, its database and the photos are hosted in the United States. An encrypted backup of the database (without photos) is kept in Finland, and an independent copy of the photos is kept in the EU. Emails are sent through an email provider.

Where data leaves the EU or UK, it is protected by the safeguards these providers offer for international transfers, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. Everything is encrypted in transit.

How long it is kept

For as long as the company running the project needs it, which for safety records can be several years. At the end of our contract with them, the data is returned to them and deleted from PenLog's live systems within 30 days. Backups expire on their normal cycle.

The independent copy of each photo is locked against deletion for 365 days from upload, so that the evidence cannot be altered, and is deleted when that lock ends.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data about you, to have it corrected, or in some cases to have it deleted or restricted. Because the records are safety evidence, the company running the project may need to keep some of them.

Please send requests to the company running the project. You can also email us at info@whitebridgemarine.com and we will pass your request on and help them answer it.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the authority in the country where you live or work, or the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH), where we are based.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
